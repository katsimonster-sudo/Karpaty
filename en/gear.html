Care & Field Repair Guide

Proven rules for extending the life of your favorite gear in Carpathian conditions.

⛺ How to dry a tent properly

Never store a tent wet for more than 24 hours — this destroys the PU/silicone coating. Dry it pitched or spread in the shade, avoiding direct sunlight.

🧥 Washing Gore-Tex membranes

Wash only with special liquid detergents (Nikwax Tech Wash or Grangers) at 30°C without spinning. Never use regular washing powders or conditioners — they clog membrane pores.

🛏️ Storing down sleeping bags

Between trips, a down sleeping bag must be stored exclusively in a large mesh storage bag, not in a compression sack, so that the down does not compress and lose its loft (Fill Power).

🩹 Emergency repair of tent pole and sleeping pad

Always carry an aluminum repair sleeve for tent poles and self-adhesive patches (Tear-Aid or Tenacious Tape). They allow you to repair a broken pole or a punctured sleeping pad on the ridge within 2 minutes.
