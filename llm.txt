# Dozhyty do finisha... (Дожити до фініша...)
# https://dozhity.space

> A personal hiking website documenting 16 mountain expeditions across the Ukrainian Carpathians (2024–2026), with gear reviews, community features, and interactive maps.

## About This Site

This is a bilingual (Ukrainian/English) personal website created by a passionate hiker who documents Carpathian mountain treks. The site name translates to "Survive to the Finish..." — a humorous take on the endurance needed for mountain hiking.

## Site Structure

- **Homepage** (`/` and `/en/`): Overview of all 16 hikes with interactive statistics, photo galleries, achievement system, and animated UI.
- **Trips** (`/trips.html` and `/en/trips.html`): Detailed chronology of 16 mountain hikes with interactive Leaflet maps, GPX tracks, elevation profiles, and per-trip statistics covering Chornohora, Gorgany, Marmarosy, Svydovets, and Borzhava mountain ranges.
- **Gear** (`/gear.html` and `/en/gear.html`): Personal catalog of hiking gear organized by category (tents, sleeping bags, footwear, cooking, electronics, first aid) with exact weights in grams and field reviews.
- **Donate** (`/donate.html` and `/en/donate.html`): Support page with Monobank jar, PrivatBank, and PayPal options for funding new expeditions and gear upgrades.
- **Community** (`/community.html` and `/en/community.html`): Bulletin board for finding hiking companions, posting trip announcements, and connecting with fellow Carpathian hikers.

## Key Content

- 16 documented hikes spanning 2024, 2025, and 2026
- Mountain ranges covered: Чорногора (Chornohora), Ґорґани (Gorgany), Мармароси (Marmarosy), Свидовець (Svydovets), Боржава (Borzhava)
- GPS tracks and routes in GPX format
- Detailed gear weight analysis (base weight, consumables, total pack weight)
- Community-driven hiking partner matching
